# School bus

The articulated bus was modeled in Blender. Its front lamp housings, headlamps, marker lights, and grille surround incorporate geometry from [Schoolbus by Poly by Google](https://poly.pizza/m/8xacyNqdJ3t), used under [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Those parts were resized, repositioned, and recolored. The coach body, interior, sliding doors, and wheels were rebuilt for this game.

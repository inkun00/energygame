# Energy village diorama

`energy_village_diorama.png` was created with the built-in imagegen tool on 2026-11-04 as a style-transfer of the existing `energy_village_map.webp`. The original is preserved. This is generated artwork, not a third-party CC0 asset. No external commercial-game art was copied.

Production prompt:

> Edit the supplied energy_village_map.webp. Preserve its 5:4 aspect ratio, isometric camera, island coastline, central river, top mountain rim, green left meadows/cliffs, golden upper-right solar meadow, central bridge/electrical plaza, middle-right residential clearing, lower-left tidal lagoon, bottom-middle safe plateau, and bottom-right geothermal terrain. Preserve building clearings and relative placement. Restyle as a bright matte low-poly toy diorama for elementary children, coherent with Kenney low-poly assets: simple faceted shapes, geometric trees, broad clear grassy surfaces, mint/teal/ochre colors, sandy paths, soft ambient daylight, shallow shadows, crisp silhouettes and reduced texture noise. No text, labels, HUD, icons, people, vehicles, generators or buildings. Keep empty usable land in every zone and the entire map visible.

The runtime uses this raster as a lightweight background. Placement pads remain separate interactive controls; a few pad centers were aligned to the new clearings. No saved-player data is created.
